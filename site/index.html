Watched wallets

Wallets join automatically when a MEDIUM/HIGH-flagged bet of theirs wins, or when they're flagged on two different matches. You can add your own. Each one is checked against its full Polymarket history: someone who keeps buying at 0.40 should win about 40% of the time, and the luck score says how far above that they are (below 1 = normal luck, above 2.5 = very unlikely to be luck). Suspect wallets add +5 points and lift their bets to MEDIUM from $500 and HIGH from $3,000; sharp bettors add +3, unclear +2, lucky whales +1. Wallets you add count as suspect.

How the plan works
  • Paper only. Starts at $300. Nothing is ever placed for real.
  • First flag: $40 on HIGH, $20 on MEDIUM, $10 on LOW, on the side the suspicious money backed.
  • Escalation (LOW → MEDIUM → HIGH before kickoff) tops up to 1.5× the new level's stake. LOW → HIGH is $60 in total.
  • LIVE: a bet placed during the match that passes a far stricter test (fresh or watched wallet, $5k+, 25%+ of the match) adds $20 on that side, never against an existing bet.
  • Every market on its own: match winner, maps, total maps, map handicap, rounds. Side markets are bet only once something in them is flagged.
  • Real prices: each bet fills against Polymarket's order book at that moment, fee included. If nobody is selling, nothing is bet.
  • Also flagged: split bets (3+ fresh wallets, $5k+ together) and line moves (the flagged side up 12c+ in the 6 hours before kickoff raises the level).
  • Bookmaker check: Pinnacle, 1xBet and Stake odds (via OddsPapi). If their chance for the flagged side moves 6+ points the same way in 12 h, the level goes up one step; the opposite way, down one step.
  • When bets go in: since Oct 19 21:26 UTC, new bets are placed in the last 30 minutes before kickoff, at the level the match has then (1.5× if the flag rose during the day). Money on these matches mostly arrives in the final hour. Bets placed earlier stay as they were.
  • Tiers from Valve's own ranking: T1 #1–20, T2 #21–50, T3 #51–100, T4 #101–200, T5 #201+, T6 unranked. Matches where both teams are top 30, or big-stage events, are skipped.
  • Who would have to throw: backing one side means the other has to lose on purpose. If that team is in Valve's top 50 (T1–T2, e.g. BIG #25), the flag is dropped and nothing is bet. If it's #51–100 (T3), the flag drops one level.
  • Share of the match is judged against kickoff: money on these matches arrives late (about 10% is in 6 hours before kickoff, half in the last hour), so a bet's share is measured against the money the match is expected to have by kickoff, never less than a typical match of its tier. An early $6,000 that is 86% of the market 12 hours out counts as about 25%.
  • Size floors: Polymarket stakes are big, so a flag needs real money: LOW from $3k, MEDIUM from $5k (or $3k if it's 30%+ of the match), HIGH from $10k (or $5k at 30%+).
  • Downgrades keep what's in. If the flagged side flips, no new money goes in. A bet the balance can't cover is skipped.
