How the odd-shift plan works
  • Paper only. A separate $300 account, started Oct 20 2026.
  • The idea: during a match the price should follow the scoreboard. When the team that is clearly ahead trades far below what the score says, someone may know it won't finish the job (your examples: Damajuana a map up at 35%, Revise 7–3 up in the decider at 19%, G2 Ares 14–12 up at 0.3%; all three lost).
  • Fair price from the score: Polymarket's live score (rounds in the current map, maps won). Each round is treated as a coin tilted by the teams' pre-match price, a map is first to 13 with overtime in blocks of 6, the series best of 1/3/5.
  • Signal: live T2–T6 match, match-winner market, the scoreboard leader is 60%+ by that model, and the market prices it at least 35 points lower.
  • Bet: $20 on the team the market prefers (the one behind on the scoreboard), once per match, at the next scan, filled against the real order book. Skipped at 0.97+.
  • Not backtested. Polymarket keeps no score history, so this rule starts untested. Every live score and price is stored from today so it can be checked properly later.
How the live plan works
  • Paper only. A separate $300 account, started Oct 20 2026. Nothing is ever placed for real.
  • Signal: during a T3–T6 match (stronger team below #50 in Valve's ranking, or unranked), one wallet has bought $4,000+ net on one side of a market after kickoff, and wallets with $2,000+ on the other side add up to less than half of that. Any market counts.
  • Bet: $20 on that side, once per market, at the next scan (within 10 minutes), filled against Polymarket's real order book with the fee. Skipped if that side already costs 0.97 or more.
  • Why live and not before kickoff: in 60 days of past matches, big pre-match bets only picked the favourite. Following them did no better than backing the favourite, and big bets on underdogs lost 15–23%. Of the pre-match settings that looked good on the first 30 days, only 30% stayed positive on the next 30.
  • The test behind it: copying $3k+ in-play bets on T3–T6 matches 10 minutes later returned +16.7% over 140 bets (+21.7% and +11.0% in the two halves). Backing the favourite at those same moments returned −6.4%. It was positive at every size from $1.5k to $5k and in 6 of 9 weeks. Most of it came from T3 matches. The rules were fixed before any live bet. Raised to $4k on Oct 21 2026: at $4k the same test gave +18.9% over 83 bets (+17% and +22% in the two halves).
  • Caveat: in-play money often reacts to the score faster than the price does, and we copy up to 10 minutes late. The live results here are the real test.
How the plan works
  • Paper only. A fresh $300 account, started Oct 20 2026. Nothing is ever placed for real.
  • Informed wallet: in its last 21 days, 5+ CS2 bets of $20+ placed before kickoff on matches that have finished, with a luck score of 2.5+ (pure luck gets there about 0.6% of the time) and wins at least 10 points above what its prices predicted.
  • Luck score: how many standard deviations a wallet's wins are above what the prices it paid predicted. Buying at 0.50 on 30 bets should win about 15; winning 25 is a luck score of about 3.7.
  • Signal: on a T3–T6 match (stronger team below #50 in Valve's ranking, or unranked), informed wallets have $200+ net on one side of a market, and less than half of that on the other side. Any market counts: match, maps, totals, rounds.
  • Bet: $20 on that side, once per market, copied as soon as the signal appears (the next scan, within 10 minutes), filled against Polymarket's real order book with the fee. No sellers, no bet. Copying early beat waiting until kickoff in the 60-day data (+18.2% vs +11.4% on the same bets): the price drifts towards the informed side once they've bet.
  • All-time profit: each informed wallet's whole Polymarket history, losing bets it never cashed in included. Several are long-run losers on a hot streak. That is shown, not filtered: in the 60-day test, following only wallets with a profitable whole CS2 history gave the same result (+17.7% vs +17.6%), and following wallets picked on their whole CS2 record did not pay at all.
  • Why these rules: in a 60-day walk-forward test (2,767 matches, 58,458 positions, no hindsight), following these wallets returned +17.5% over 55 bets and was positive in both halves. Without the $200 condition the edge disappeared. These settings were fixed before any live bet, so the live results are a fair test.
  • Data: Polymarket only. Tiers come from Valve's Regional Standings, refreshed daily.
